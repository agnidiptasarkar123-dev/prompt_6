## 1. Government Publications & Standards

### 1.1 Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) NAQI (2014)
- **Publication:** *National Air Quality Index*, Central Pollution Control Board, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India, October 2014 (Publication No. CUPS/82/2014-15).
- **Scope of Use:** Official statutory breakpoint tables (Table 3.11), sub-index interpolation formulas, 16-hour statutory completeness rules, and 8-pollutant monitoring parameters.
- **Licensing Notice:** Official government statutory publication. Not licensed under Apache-2.0. Referenced for domain correctness under statutory public governance.

### 1.2 MoHFW / National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) Health Advisory
- **Publication:** *National Programme on Climate Change and Human Health (NPCCHH)*, National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC), Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India (October 2025 Advisory).
- **Scope of Use:** Public health advisory action triggers, vulnerable sub-population classifications, diurnal outdoor exercise restrictions during winter peak inversions (06:00–09:30 and 18:00–22:00), and indoor combustion safety rules.
- **Licensing Notice:** Official public health guidance issued for citizen protection. Not licensed under Apache-2.0.
