# HawaSaathi — Phase 6 Controlled Real-World Pilot Plan

**Document ID:** `HS-PLN-P6-PILOT-2026`\
**Classification:** Operational Pilot Deployment Plan\
**Target Duration:** 4 Weeks\

---

## 1. Pilot Scope & Geographical Boundaries

To ensure scientific defensibility, the pilot is restricted to metropolitan regions with high-density physical CAAQMS monitoring infrastructure on `data.gov.in`:

1. **Delhi National Capital Region (NCR):** 40+ active monitoring stations (Anand Vihar, Punjabi Bagh, RK Puram, IGI Airport, Alipur). Represents acute particulate spikes and severe atmospheric inversions.
2. **Kolkata Metropolitan Area (West Bengal):** 7+ active monitoring stations (Victoria Memorial, Fort William, Rabindra Bharati, Ballygunge). Represents high humidity, coastal-delta dispersion, and particulate variations.
3. **Bengaluru Urban (Karnataka):** 10+ active monitoring stations (BTM Layout, Peenya, Silk Board, City Railway Station). Represents traffic corridors and moderate diurnal elevation profiles.

*Explicit Pilot Exclusions:* Areas without active CAAQMS stations (e.g. Lakshadweep, Ladakh) are excluded from live pilot cohort evaluations and receive model forecasts only.

---

## 2. Participant Cohorts & Informed Consent

- **Cohort Size:** 75 participants across the 3 pilot cities (25 per city).
- **Cohort Diversity:**
  - Daily commuters (metro, bus, auto, two-wheeler, pedestrians).
  - Parents and household decision-makers.
  - Individuals with self-reported environmental respiratory sensitivities.
- **Informed Consent Protocol (DPDP Act 2023):**
  - Explicit consent screen prior to voluntary participation.
  - Transparent disclosure: health flags are non-diagnostic, optional, and stored only in client session or encrypted profile.
  - Instant 1-click opt-out and cascading account wipe.
